Autoscroll-to-footer happens because navigation keeps the old scroll position instead of resetting to top. In the category pages, `router.push(..., { scroll: false })` preserves scroll state (see `src/app/(shop)/categories/[slug]/CategoryClient.tsx` and `src/app/(shop)/categories/CategoriesClient.tsx`). When a new page or skeleton renders with shorter height, the browser clamps the preserved scroll position to the bottom, so you land near the footer. This is why the new page doesn’t start at the top.
